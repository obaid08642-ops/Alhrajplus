# Phase 10 — competitor source notes

## Haraj official web

Direct inspection of `https://haraj.com.sa/en/` showed a prominent search box, login, language and theme controls, a broad category rail including Cars, Devices, Real Estate, Furniture, Services, Fashion, Animals, Jobs, Games, Coaching, Food, Events, Coding, Gardens, Rarities, Projects and investments, Arts, Trips, Tourism, and Lost & Found. It also exposes brand shortcuts, region shortcuts, a Near control, and listing feeds. The page presents rich car subcategories, device brands, animal types, and real-estate subtypes.

## OpenSooq official web

Direct inspection of `https://www.opensooq.com/en` redirected to a localized country homepage. It showed city and category selectors, search, favorites, my posts, notifications, login, Add Listing, a large category taxonomy, Reviews & Ratings, Sell Now, newly listed discovery, brand/city/year tabs for vehicles, and localized SEO landing sections. The homepage also markets a “Snap Photo. List It. Sell It.” flow and a “Search. Call. Buy.” flow.

## Implications for Alhrajplus

Alhrajplus already has search history/trending/suggestions, country isolation, favorites, notifications, AI image search, structured listing creation, review/comment routes, and Admin analytics. The verified competitor gaps to prioritize are a broader and more visibly discoverable category taxonomy, brand/city/year landing filters, explicit reviews-and-ratings entry points, a native snap-photo listing flow on Mobile, and stronger SEO landing coverage by category/brand/city. These observations are source-backed but do not imply that every competitor internal feature was observable from a public homepage.
